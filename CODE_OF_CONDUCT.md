# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and accept responsibility for mistakes.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sexualized attention, and
other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily
or permanently ban participants whose behavior violates this code. Report incidents
confidentially through a private security advisory at
<https://github.com/cmm-cmm/Hamster-Server/security/advisories/new>. Reports will be
reviewed promptly, fairly, and with respect for the reporter's privacy.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, available at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html>.
